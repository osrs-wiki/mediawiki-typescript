A '''world''' is an instance of ''[[Old School RuneScape]]'' that can be joined in order to play the game. These are sometimes referred to as '''servers''' (though multiple worlds can be hosted on the same physical server), or shortened to '''W''' (e.g. "Hop to W301"). Each members world allows 2000 players to be connected at once while free-to-play worlds have a limit of 1400 players at once.{{CiteDiscord|author=Mod Ash|url=https://discord.com/channels/324132423636090880/431834766325841920/1437393904713597030|date=10 November 2025|archiveurl=https://reldo.runescape.wiki/i/27962804#chat-messages-431834766325841920-1437393904713597030|archivedate=7 March 2026|quote=The cap's been going up again since then. Didn't really need to be so low. I think it's 1.4k at present, creeping up over time.}}

==Isolation of worlds==
For the most part, events that occur in one world will not affect any other ''RuneScape'' world. For example, buying an item in a [[shop]] will only decrease the inventory for the shop in that world. Shop inventory, character [[respawn]]s, [[item respawns]], and resource regeneration (e.g. [[ores]], [[trees]]) are all managed individually for each world. Additionally, players (along with chat messages) contained in one world will not be visible to players in another world.

One exception to this rule is that multiple worlds hosted on the same physical server may share timings for certain events. For example, when [[tackling organised crime]], each world will consistently spawn and despawn the gang meetings at the same times and locations as the other worlds on that server.

===World hopping===
{{Main|World switching}}
Because each world has its own [[shop]] inventory, players will often change worlds rapidly to buy from one store many different times. Also, players often switch worlds to find more favourable conditions (e.g. more populated) for [[minigames]], [[player killing]], or [[trading]]. This is known as ''[[world hopping]]''.

==Interaction between worlds==
The only possible ways of interaction between ''RuneScape'' worlds is [[private chat]] using the [[Friends List]] or through [[Clan Chat]].

Additionally, offers placed on the Grand Exchange can be fulfilled across any world.

==Persistence of character state==
When a user logs off of a world, all settings particular to that character are saved to the player's account [[profile]] in a global database. Therefore, when logging into a different world, the character's [[skills]], [[inventory]], dropped untradeable items such as [[clue scroll]]s, etc. will be restored to the same state, regardless of which world the character last logged onto.

==Themed worlds==
In August 2007, [[Jagex]] added a new column to the world select screen highlighting those worlds with associated activities, especially those team-based events like [[Trouble Brewing]], to make it easier to find people with similar ideas. Some of these worlds have special mechanics.

===Skill total worlds===
Skill total worlds are worlds that are locked to accounts that do not meet the required [[Total level]] to enter. [[Free-to-play]] skill total worlds calculate total levels from free-to-play [[skills]] alone and do not acknowledge [[members]] skills, meaning players require a cumulative total of 500 or 750 in free-to-play skills (total level of 509 or 759 respectively if all member's skills are 1) to enter those worlds. Members skill total worlds were added on [[20 August]] 2015. The free-to-play skill total worlds were added on [[22 September]] 2016.

===High-risk worlds===
'''High Risk worlds''' (worlds 365, 474, 533, PvP worlds 548 and 579) are worlds where players will lose '''all''' items on [[PvP]] death due to having a special skull status over the head ([[File:Skull (High Risk PvP world) icon.png]]) at all times. The Protect Item prayer does not work on these worlds. Going into these worlds can be a [[risk]], but good way to [[Money making guide|make money]] if you have a high [[Combat level]] with good [[armour]] and [[weapons]] and are proficient in [[player-versus-player]] [[combat]]. '''It is strongly recommended that you only take items that you are willing to lose!''' Also, note that World 365, 474, and 533 are safe worlds '''unless you go into the [[Wilderness]]''' as they are not [[PvP]] worlds.

====PvP Worlds====
{{main|PvP world}}
On these worlds, players may attack each other almost everywhere and are prevented from teleporting for a few seconds after using a special attack in PvP combat, similarly to PvP combat in within the [[Wilderness]] on regular worlds. This is done to prevent players from using their special attack on a player and immediately teleporting away without consequence. Players are also prevented from teleporting while their special attack bar is activated on these worlds.

Upon logging into a [[PvP world]] while in a safe area, a chat message will appear saying {{mes|colour=red|This is a PvP World. Players may attack you once you leave this safe area.}}
Logging in while in a PvP-enabled area instead gives the chat message {{mes|colour=red|This is a PvP world. Players may attack you almost anywhere.}}

====Wilderness PK Worlds====
These worlds are meant to facilitate finding fights in the [[Wilderness]], but otherwise have no additional mechanics compared to a regular world.

===Minigames===
====Blast Furnace Worlds====
On these worlds, players cannot operate the mechanics of the [[Blast Furnace]]. Instead, five dwarves operate the Blast Furnace for a fee of {{NoCoins|72000|c}} per hour, allowing for players to purely focus on placing ores and collecting bars.

====Bounty Hunter Worlds====
Players can play the [[Bounty Hunter]] minigame at [[Daimon's Crater]] only on these worlds. Additionally, old [[PJ timer]] mechanics apply in [[Single-way combat]] zones of the Wilderness; upon logging into a Bounty Hunter world, a chat message will appear saying {{mes|In these worlds, attacking a player will not increase your PJ timer.}} [[Vesta's longsword (bh)]] and [[Vesta's blighted longsword]] can be used in the Wilderness and [[Ancient artefact]]s can be exchanged for coins by speaking to the [[Emblem Trader]] in the [[Revenant Caves]] on Bounty Hunter worlds.

====Brimhaven Agility Arena Worlds====
On these worlds, the first player to tag the currently marked [[Ticket Dispenser|dispenser]] gets their name displayed as a chat message for everyone in the arena. There are no additional rewards.

====Castle Wars Worlds====
Players will receive one extra [[Castle wars ticket|Castle Wars ticket]] per game on these worlds, regardless of outcome. [[Plaudits]] are awarded, equal to the number of tickets won.
Various mechanics are different for these worlds:
* The Saradomin and Zamorak portals cannot be entered, only the Guthix portal is available.
* Games start 5 minutes after the previous round has ended, as opposed to 2 minutes on regular worlds.
* Placing barricades requires a total level of 500, and a maximum of 17 barricades (7 outside the base and 10 inside) can be set up.
* On '''non-dedicated''' worlds, the usual 6-tick immunity after being stunned with the [[dragon spear]] or [[Zamorakian hasta]] special attacks is not applied inside Castle Wars.

====Guardians of the Rift Worlds====
On regular worlds, players can speak to [[Apprentice Tamara]] to lock the [[Barrier (Temple of the Eye)|barrier]], preventing other players from entering after the rift opens. This '''cannot''' be done on the dedicated Guardians of the Rift worlds.

====LMS Competitive Worlds====
Players can earn [[Last Man Standing]] points and ranks only on these worlds. There are no additional mechanics in the Wilderness.

* World 330 is the American world for house parties and player-owned gilded altars. Along with world 302, it is usually the most populated world in the game during North American daytime and evening hours.
* World 331 is the unofficial US West world for house parties and player-owned gilded altars

==Technical details==
Each world is a {{WP|Containerization_(computing)|containerised}} virtual machine running the [[Java|Java-based]] world application.{{CiteNews|title=Behind The Scenes of OSRS Maintenance|url=https://secure.runescape.com/m=news/a=13/behind-the-scenes-of-osrs-maintenance?oldschool=1|newsdate=15 July 2025}} Though worlds used to run on dedicated physical machines, virtualisation was sought to minimise hardware-related downtime and thus offline maintenance. At this time, world server containers run on a co-scheduled CPU scheduling platform known as {{WP|VMware ESXi|ESXi}}. That said, Jagex has made plans to migrate to an asynchronous scheduler (Linux CFS/PreemptRT tuning under KVM) sometime in the future in order to further improve tick performance.

The replacement included a free-to-play Sailing alpha icon, despite the alpha and beta only being accessible to members.
